How do I start learning forex trading?+

Start with the fundamentals — currency pairs, candlestick patterns and market structure — before risking any capital. Our blog walks through this step by step, and the Basic to Intermediate course builds on it with live instruction.

Is Equity Fin Academy good for beginners?+

Yes. The Basic to Intermediate track is built for traders with no prior experience, starting from forex fundamentals before moving into structure and strategy.

Do you teach anything besides forex?+

No. Equity Fin Academy teaches forex exclusively — one connected market-structure framework, taught in depth, rather than a shallow overview spread across multiple asset classes.
