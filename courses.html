COURSE FAQS

Questions about the curriculum

Which track should I start with?+

If you're new to trading, start with Basic to Intermediate — it covers forex fundamentals, candlestick patterns and market structure. If you already understand these, Advanced Institutional Strategies goes straight into order flow and institutional tools.

Is this course only about forex?+

Yes. Both tracks focus exclusively on forex, taught as one connected market-structure framework rather than spread thin across other asset classes.

What is "Lifetime Support via WhatsApp"?+

Students on the Advanced Institutional Strategies track get ongoing access to the instructor via WhatsApp for questions after the course ends.

Is broker access included in the course fee?+

Practical sessions are run through Star Trader, an SCA-regulated broker. See Pricing or call us for current enrolment and account details.
