TEACHING PHILOSOPHY

Three principles behind every lesson

01

Structure over indicators

Market structure, order flow and liquidity are taught before any indicator — because indicators describe price, structure explains it.

02

Risk before reward

Position sizing and trading psychology are covered as core curriculum, not an afterthought bolted onto strategy lessons.

03

Depth over breadth

One market, mastered properly, beats a shallow overview of many. Every lesson goes deeper into forex, not wider into other assets.
