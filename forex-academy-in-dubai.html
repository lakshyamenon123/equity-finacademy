WHY LEARN FOREX IN DUBAI

Dubai is a genuinely good place to learn forex trading.

Not just because it's where we happen to be based — a few practical reasons this location actually helps.

A timezone that overlaps two major sessions

Dubai's GMT+4 timezone sits within reach of both the London and Asian trading sessions, useful for observing how forex liquidity shifts through the day.

Access to SCA-regulated brokers

The UAE's Securities and Commodities Authority regulates brokers operating locally, giving Dubai-based traders access to properly overseen platforms for practice trading.

A large, active trading community

Dubai's international, finance-heavy population means forex isn't a niche interest here — there's a real community of traders at every level to learn alongside.

An in-person option, not just a screen

Most forex education is purely online. Equity Fin Academy runs live, in-person classes in Al Qusais — direct feedback, real accountability, no muted webinar chat.
